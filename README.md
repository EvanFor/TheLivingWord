# TheLivingWord

**Bringing biblical stories to life through cinematic AI filmmaking.**

TheLivingWord is a long-term creative project exploring biblical stories through thoughtful, cinematic, AI-assisted film production.

The repository preserves creative decisions and production continuity across independent films: scripture research, script drafts, storyboards, character and location references, prompts, shot plans, and production notes.

## Current films

- **The Look (那一眼)** — Peter's denial of Jesus, the rooster's crow, and the moment Jesus turns and looks at Peter.
- **The Sea of Tiberias (提比哩亚海边)** — the disciples' fruitless night of fishing, the miraculous catch, breakfast by the charcoal fire, Jesus' three questions to Peter, and the renewed call to follow.

## Project principles

- Treat the biblical text with care; distinguish direct scripture quotations from adaptation.
- Build story logic, emotional intention, and shot continuity before generating footage.
- Maintain consistent character, costume, location, lighting, and screen direction across shots.
- Prefer restrained, believable performances and cinematic realism over visual spectacle.
- Track revisions so approved decisions do not silently drift.
- Keep each film independent unless an explicit creative decision connects them.

# 中文版 / Chinese Version

# TheLivingWord

**通过电影化的 AI 影像创作，让圣经故事重新鲜活。**

TheLivingWord 是一个长期创作项目，致力于以审慎、电影化的叙事方式，借助 AI 辅助制作呈现圣经故事。仓库用于保存多部独立影片的创作决定与制作连续性，包括经文研究、剧本版本、分镜、人物与场景参考、提示词、镜头计划和制作记录。

## 当前影片

- **The Look（那一眼）**——彼得三次不认主、鸡鸣，以及耶稣转身看彼得的那一刻。
- **The Sea of Tiberias（提比哩亚海边）**——门徒整夜捕鱼、一网丰收、炭火旁的早餐、耶稣三问彼得，以及重新发出的跟从呼召。

## 项目原则

- 尊重圣经文本，清楚区分经文直接引用与电影改编。
- 在生成影像前，先建立故事逻辑、情绪意图和镜头连续性。
- 保持人物、服装、场景、光线和屏幕方向的一致。
- 相较视觉奇观，优先追求克制、可信的表演与电影真实感。
- 对创作决定进行版本管理，避免已批准的内容在后续工作中悄然偏移。
- 各影片保持独立，除非明确作出跨影片关联的创作决定。
