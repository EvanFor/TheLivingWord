# The Look — Storyboard

**Status:** Draft outline; shot timings and staging are not locked.  
**Target runtime:** approximately 90 seconds.  
**Aspect ratio:** 16:9.

## Sequence outline

| Sequence | Approx. time | Dramatic function |
|---|---:|---|
| The declaration | 00:00–00:18 | Establish Peter's conviction and Jesus' warning |
| Three denials | 00:18–00:58 | Escalating fear and defensive reactions |
| The rooster | 00:58–01:04 | A sharp sound cue breaks the scene |
| The look | 01:04–01:20 | Jesus turns; Peter meets his eyes |
| Peter leaves | 01:20–01:30 | Recognition gives way to grief |

## Storyboard principles

- The third denial is not the emotional climax; the look is.
- Allow a beat of silence after the rooster before the look.
- Hold the eye contact long enough for the audience to read it, without artificial slow motion.
- Peter should not begin crying instantly. Show the progression from shock to recognition to collapse.
- Keep the courtyard geography and the positions of Peter, Jesus, the fire, and the passage through the courtyard consistent.
- Use shot IDs (for example, `TL-001`) in prompts, generated assets, and the shot tracker.

Detailed shot-by-shot boards will be added after the script and courtyard blocking are reviewed.
