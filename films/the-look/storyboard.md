## Continuity map

### Courtyard geography
- The fire is the fixed anchor in the foreground-middle of the master shot.
- Peter approaches from the passage side and settles near the fire.
- The servant woman and the two men are positioned around the fire; their exact marks are to be fixed in the location plan.
- Jesus is escorted across the far side of the same courtyard at the moment of the look.
- Peter exits through the passage he entered from, unless the final location plan establishes a clearer exit. Do not reverse the geography between shots.

### Screen direction and eyelines
- Establish the courtyard master before close-ups.
- Keep Peter's eyeline toward the same off-screen passage when he glances toward Jesus.
- Jesus' turn and Peter's reaction must match on the same axis.
- Do not mirror or horizontally flip generated shots to repair a continuity error without checking costume, fire placement, and screen direction.

### Light and color
- Upper room: warm, low practical lamp light; intimate, restrained.
- Courtyard: night ambience in cool-neutral shadows, with natural amber firelight on faces.
- Avoid making the entire courtyard blue. Firelight should feel physically motivated, not orange fantasy glow.
- The rooster cue does not cause a lighting change.

## Editorial rules

- Do not cut at equal intervals mechanically; preserve the timing of each actor's reaction.
- The first and second denials should be relatively quick.
- Give the third denial slightly more space because the pressure has accumulated.
- The rooster is a sound-led transition.
- The look receives the most deliberate uninterrupted screen time.
- Avoid explanatory flashbacks and repeated memory voice-over. The opening prediction is sufficient; let Peter's reaction carry remembrance.

## Items to lock before image generation

1. Final courtyard top-down layout and camera axis.
2. Character positions and costume continuity.
3. Whether TL-014 to TL-017 can be generated as one coherent movement or should be produced as separate controlled shots.
4. Final total runtime after an animatic.

## 连戏地图 / Continuity map

### 院落空间
- 火堆是主镜头中前景区域的固定空间锚点。
- 彼得从通道一侧进入，随后在火堆附近坐下。
- 女仆和两名男子分布在火堆周围；具体站位需在场景平面图中锁定。
- 那一眼发生时，耶稣从同一院落的远侧经过。
- 除非最终场景图明确设定更清晰的出口，否则彼得从进入时的通道离开。镜头之间不得反转空间关系。

### 屏幕方向与视线
- 先建立院落全景，再拍摄近景。
- 彼得望向耶稣时，视线必须指向同一侧的画外通道。
- 耶稣回头与彼得反应必须保持同一轴线。
- 不得为了修复连戏错误就直接水平翻转生成画面；必须检查服装、火堆位置和屏幕方向。

### 光线与色彩
- 楼上房间：温暖、低照度的实用油灯光，亲密而克制。
- 院落：夜间环境保持冷中性阴影，人物脸上有自然的琥珀色火光。
- 不要让整个院子变成蓝色；火光应符合真实光源，不要变成幻想式橙色光晕。
- 鸡鸣不引起任何光线变化。

## 剪辑规则 / Editorial rules

- 不要机械地按相同间隔剪辑；保留演员反应所需的时间。
- 第一次和第二次否认相对快速。
- 第三次否认应稍微留出空间，因为压力已经累积。
- 鸡鸣是由声音引导的转场。
- 那一眼应获得全片最有意识、最完整的连续停留时间。
- 避免解释性闪回和重复的记忆旁白。开场的预言已经足够，让彼得的反应承载记忆。

## 正式生成图片前需要锁定的事项

1. 院落最终俯视布局与摄影轴线。
2. 人物位置与服装连续性。
3. TL-014 至 TL-017 是生成一段连贯动作，还是拆分为可控的独立镜头。
4. 动态分镜完成后的最终总时长。
