# The Look — Animatic & Asset Register / 《那一眼》动态分镜与资产登记

**Version:** 1.0  
**Status:** Production planning / 制作规划

## 1. Animatic purpose / 动态分镜目的

The animatic is the final timing and continuity test before video generation. Use temporary stills, simple pans/crops, scratch dialogue, a temporary rooster cue and rough ambience. It is not a visual-quality test and does not require final character art.

动态分镜是正式生成视频前最后一次时长与连续性测试。使用临时静帧、简单推拉／裁切、临时对白、鸡鸣和粗略环境声即可。它不是画质测试，不需要最终人物定妆图。

## 3. Animatic review / 动态分镜审阅

1. Can the audience follow the time jump without an explanatory title?
2. Are the three denials distinct and escalating?
3. Is the approximately one-hour interval before the third accusation suggested clearly but economically?
4. Does the rooster overlap the final denial?
5. Is Jesus visibly in the same courtyard, with a believable path and matching eyeline?
6. Does the four-second look feel sufficient rather than slow?
7. Does Peter's grief begin only after recognition?
8. Can the film end without narration or music?

1. 观众能否在没有解释性字幕的情况下理解时间跳跃？
2. 三次否认是否有清晰区别并逐渐升级？
3. 第三次指认前约一小时的间隔，是否通过简洁的视觉方式清楚暗示？
4. 鸡鸣是否与最后一次否认重叠？
5. 耶稣是否明确处于同一院落，路线可信且视线匹配？
6. 四秒对视是否足够有力，而不显拖沓？
7. 彼得是否在意识到真相之后才开始显露悲痛？
8. 影片能否在没有旁白和音乐的情况下结束？

## 5. Shot status rules / 镜头状态规则

- planned: prompt and dependencies defined; no generation.
- in-progress: at least one take generated.
- review: candidate take awaits sequence review.
- approved: identity, continuity, performance, duration and technical quality pass.
- blocked: a required reference or decision is missing.
- superseded: replaced by another take or decision; retain history.

- planned：提示词和依赖已明确，尚未生成。
- in-progress：至少生成过一条素材。
- review：已有候选素材，等待放入时间线审阅。
- approved：身份、连续性、表演、时长与技术质量全部通过。
- blocked：缺少必要参考或决定，暂时无法继续。
- superseded：已被其他素材或决定替代，保留历史记录。
