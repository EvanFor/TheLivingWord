# The Look — Production Bible / 《那一眼》制作总纲

**Version / 版本:** 1.0  
**Status / 状态:** Production-ready proposal; approvals still required / 可执行制作提案；仍需创作审批  
**Runtime / 时长:** 90 seconds / 90秒  
**Frame / 画幅:** 16:9 horizontal / 16:9横向  
**Primary text / 主要经文:** Luke 22:31–34, 54–62 / 路加福音22:31–34、54–62

## 1. Film identity / 影片定位

**Logline:** On the night Jesus is arrested, Peter's confident promise collapses under fear. As the rooster crows, Jesus turns and looks at him—and Peter remembers.

**一句话梗概：** 耶稣被捕的夜晚，彼得曾经坚定的承诺在恐惧中崩塌。鸡鸣响起，耶稣转身看向他，彼得想起了那句话。

**Dramatic center / 戏剧核心:** The look in Luke 22:61—not the denial as spectacle, and not grief as performance. / 路加福音22:61中的那一眼——不是把否认拍成奇观，也不是把悲痛演成表演。

**Audience experience / 观众体验:** The audience understands the prediction before the courtyard sequence, recognizes the rooster as its fulfillment, and feels the weight of the look without being told what emotion to assign to Jesus. / 观众应在院落段落之前听懂预言，在鸡鸣时意识到预言应验，并在那一眼中感受到重量，而不是被告知耶稣必须表现出哪一种情绪。

## 2. Canonical story decisions / 核心叙事决定

| Decision | Production direction |
|---|---|
| Gospel account | Luke is the controlling narrative source. Do not silently harmonize other Gospels into Luke. |
| Opening | Luke 22:33–34, earlier that night. |
| First denial | Peter says he does not know Jesus (22:57). |
| Second denial | Preserve the short answer “I am not” (22:58). |
| Third accusation | Retain the Galilean identification (22:59); indicate the approximately one-hour interval before it, compressed visually. Do not claim Luke says accent exposed Peter. |
| Rooster | Off-screen; crow overlaps Peter still speaking (22:60). |
| Look | Jesus turns and looks at Peter; Peter remembers (22:61). |
| Exit | Peter goes outside and weeps bitterly (22:62). |
| Memory voice-over | Remove it from the recommended cut. The opening prediction already supplies the memory. |
| Ending | No explanatory narration; end on Peter's departure / empty fire space, then a restrained scripture reference card. |

| 决定 | 制作方向 |
|---|---|
| 福音书依据 | 以路加福音为叙事主线，不得不加说明地把其他福音书的细节混入路加。 |
| 开场 | 路加福音22:33–34，当晚较早时候。 |
| 第一次否认 | 彼得说自己不认识耶稣（22:57）。 |
| 第二次否认 | 保留简短回答“我不是”（22:58）。 |
| 第三次指认 | 保留加利利人的身份指认（22:59）；以视觉方式暗示此前约一小时的间隔，并进行时间压缩。不要说路加记载彼得因口音暴露。 |
| 鸡鸣 | 画外音；与彼得仍在说话的尾声重叠（22:60）。 |
| 那一眼 | 耶稣转身看彼得，彼得想起主的话（22:61）。 |
| 离开 | 彼得出去痛哭（22:62）。 |
| 记忆旁白 | 推荐剪辑中删除。开场已经交代预言，让彼得的表情与声音衔接承载记忆。 |
| 结尾 | 不加解释性旁白；以彼得离开或空下来的火堆空间结束，再出现克制的经文出处字幕。 |

## 3. Scripture and dialogue policy / 经文与对白规范

1. Luke 22:31 uses a plural address; Luke 22:32 turns to Peter personally. Preserve that distinction in any full reading.
2. All current screenplay dialogue is adapted paraphrase, not a verbatim quotation from a selected Chinese Bible edition.
3. Do not place adapted dialogue in quotation marks on screen as though it were a published translation.
4. Use natural spoken Chinese for performance. If a verbatim Bible quotation is required later, select the edition and verify exact wording before recording or subtitle lock.
5. Recommended end card: 路加福音 22:62 / Luke 22:62 only. Do not print verse wording until the translation policy is approved.

1. 路加福音22:31使用复数称呼，22:32转为直接对彼得说话；完整呈现时必须保留这一差别。
2. 当前剧本对白均为经文基础上的改编意译，不是某个已选定中文圣经译本的逐字引用。
3. 不得把改编对白加上引号放在画面上，让观众误以为是某个正式译本原句。
4. 表演对白以自然中文为主。若之后需要逐字引用圣经，必须先确定译本，并在录音和字幕锁定前核对原文。
5. 推荐片尾只标注“路加福音 22:62 / Luke 22:62”。译本政策获批前，不显示经文逐字译文。

| 时间 | 时长 | 内容 |

| 00:18–00:58 | 40秒 | 院落中的三次否认 |

The animatic must make the approximately one-hour interval before the third accusation legible through a subtle visual time ellipsis, and protect at least four uninterrupted seconds of actual eye contact. If the cut runs long, trim transitions or non-essential movement before shortening the look.

动态分镜必须通过轻微的视觉时间跳跃，让观众感受到第三次指认前约一小时的间隔；同时保护至少四秒不间断的实际对视。如果总时长超出，先压缩转场或非必要走位，不要先缩短那一眼。

## 5. Performance direction / 表演方向

### Peter / 彼得
- Opening: sincere, direct, emotionally open; no swagger.
- First denial: reflexive self-protection; quick answer.
- Second denial: defensive irritation; wants the question to end.
- Third denial: fear leaks through; urgent, almost trying to silence the accuser.
- Rooster: body stops before the face changes.
- Look: recognition, then shame, then grief. No instant collapse.
- Exit: tries to leave with composure; breaks down after leaving the group.

### Jesus / 耶稣
- Opening: calm, direct, grounded authority.
- Courtyard: restrained under arrest; no theatrical suffering added for effect.
- Turn: small, readable physical turn, not a dramatic snap.
- Expression: open to interpretation; do not force anger, contempt, tears, or a smile.
- Eye contact: steady and human; no supernatural effects.

### 配角
- 女仆和两名男子是普通人，不是反派。
- 质问从观察逐渐变得肯定；不安排攻击性围逼。
- 背景人物数量少且位置稳定。

## 6. Visual system / 视觉系统

- Photorealistic, restrained first-century Judea/Galilee environment.
- Natural skin texture; plausible woven linen and wool, sandals, oil lamp, stone and fire.
- Upper room: intimate warm practical oil-lamp light, natural falloff, simple wood and stone.
- Courtyard: cool-neutral night shadows with physically motivated amber firelight; do not make the whole image blue or orange.
- Palette: limestone, charcoal, muted earth, undyed linen, subdued brown; fire is the only strong warm accent.
- Mostly locked camera or restrained movement; no aggressive handheld, whip pans, speed ramps, artificial lens flares.
- Foreground fire / midground people / background passage; maintain geography.
- Every generated still and video shot: 16:9 horizontal.

- 第一世纪犹太地区的真人写实历史环境。
- 自然皮肤质感；符合时代的亚麻、羊毛、凉鞋、油灯、石材与火光。
- 楼上房间：亲密、温暖的实用油灯光，暗部自然衰减，简单木材与石材。
- 院落：冷中性夜间阴影与符合物理逻辑的琥珀色火光；不要整体偏蓝或偏橙。
- 色彩：石灰岩、炭灰、低饱和大地色、原色亚麻、沉静棕色；火光是唯一明显的暖色点缀。
- 以固定机位和极克制运动为主；不用激烈手持、甩镜、变速或人造镜头光晕。
- 前景火堆／中景人物／远景通道；空间关系必须稳定。
- 所有生成静帧和视频镜头均为16:9横向。

## 7. Character design proposal — not approved / 人物设计提案——尚未批准

**Peter:** adult first-century Jewish/Galilean man; casting proposal late 30s to mid-40s only; sun-weathered natural skin, practical build, dark textured hair, ordinary slightly uneven beard, muted brown/earth robe and simple outer layer.

**Jesus:** adult first-century Jewish man; casting proposal early-to-mid 30s only; natural Middle Eastern features and skin texture, dark hair and beard, undyed or light earth-tone tunic and simple outer garment. Avoid the polished European devotional-art template.

**Supporting characters:** distinct ordinary faces, simple period clothing, no villain styling.

These are visual proposals, not claims about historical appearance. No face is final until its reference is approved.

**彼得：** 第一世纪犹太／加利利成年男性；年龄暂建议三十多岁后段至四十多岁中段，仅为选角提案；自然日晒肤色、实用结实体态、深色有纹理头发、普通略显凌乱的胡须，低饱和棕色长袍与简单外衣。

**耶稣：** 第一世纪犹太成年男性；年龄暂建议三十岁上下，仅为选角提案；自然中东地域面部特征与皮肤质感，深色头发和胡须，未染色或浅大地色长袍与简单外衣。避免欧洲宗教画中过度理想化的模板。

**配角：** 面孔彼此有区别，服装朴素符合时代，不采用反派造型。

以上只是视觉提案，不是对圣经人物真实外貌的断言。用户批准人物参考图之前，不锁定任何面孔。

## 8. Location proposal — not approved / 场景设计提案——尚未批准

### Courtyard / 院落
- Compact stone courtyard legible in one master shot.
- Main passage at rear-left of the master frame.
- Fire near center-left foreground/midground, not blocking the exit.
- Peter enters and exits through the same passage.
- Servant woman near the fire group; first man offset right; second man approaches from the far side.
- Jesus is escorted across the far side behind the fire group, on a route allowing a natural turn toward Peter.
- A clear 180-degree axis defines all reverse angles.

### Upper room / 楼上房间
- Small, modest room; low table; oil lamp; no ornate religious décor.
- Jesus and Peter face each other at short distance; other disciples are soft background silhouettes.
- The lamp motivates the warm light; no beams or halo.

### 院落
- 紧凑石砌院落，一个主镜头即可读懂空间。
- 主通道位于主镜头画面后方偏左。
- 火堆位于中前景偏左，不得挡住出口。
- 彼得从同一通道进入并离开。
- 女仆在火堆人群附近；第一名男子偏右；第二名男子从远侧靠近。
- 耶稣被押着从院落远侧、火堆人群后方经过，路线允许他自然转向彼得。
- 以清晰的180度轴线定义所有反打镜头。

### 楼上房间
- 小而朴素的房间；矮桌、油灯；不出现华丽宗教装饰。
- 耶稣与彼得近距离相对而坐；其他门徒仅作为虚化背景轮廓。
- 暖光由油灯驱动；不出现光柱或光环。

## 9. Production order / 制作顺序

1. Approve story and visual direction.
2. Approve Peter and Jesus face references.
3. Approve courtyard top-down layout and upper-room layout.
4. Approve supporting-character references and costume continuity.
5. Build a timed animatic with temporary stills and scratch audio.
6. Test TL-014–TL-017 as a continuity sequence before full generation.
7. Generate remaining shots in controlled batches using the shot prompt pack.
8. Edit, sound mix, subtitle, quality-check, and export.

1. 批准故事与视觉方向。
2. 批准彼得与耶稣的人物面孔参考。
3. 批准院落俯视布局与楼上房间布局。
4. 批准配角参考与服装连续性。
5. 使用临时静帧和临时声音制作动态分镜。
6. 完整生成前，先测试TL-014至TL-017的连续性。
7. 使用镜头提示词包分批生成其余镜头。
8. 完成剪辑、混音、字幕、质量检查和导出。
