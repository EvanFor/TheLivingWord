# The Look — Sound, Edit, Subtitles & Delivery / 《那一眼》声音、剪辑、字幕与交付

**Version:** 1.0  
**Status:** Production plan / 制作方案

## 1. Sound concept / 声音概念

Sound should make the courtyard feel inhabited, not designed as a horror space. The rooster is the decisive cue. Silence is created by reducing layers, not by inserting an artificial digital vacuum.

声音应让院落像真实有人生活的空间，而不是恐怖场景。鸡鸣是决定性提示。沉默通过减少声音层次实现，不通过人为制造数字真空。

| Track | Content | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| A1 | Dialogue | Clean, intimate, centered |
| A2 | Courtyard ambience | Night air, distant human presence, subtle movement |
| A3 | Fire | Natural crackle, stable perspective |
| A4 | Rooster | One clear off-screen crow overlapping Peter's final denial |
| A5 | Foley | Cloth, sandals on stone, restrained footsteps, breath |
| A6 | Music | Recommended: no score in the 90-second cut |

| 音轨 | 内容 | 说明 |
|---|---|---|
| A1 | 对白 | 清晰、亲密、居中 |
| A2 | 院落环境声 | 夜间空气、远处人声、细微活动 |
| A3 | 火焰 | 自然燃烧声，空间视角稳定 |
| A4 | 鸡鸣 | 一次清晰画外鸡鸣，与彼得最后一次否认重叠 |
| A5 | 拟音 | 衣料、凉鞋踩石地、克制脚步与呼吸 |
| A6 | 音乐 | 推荐90秒正片完全不配乐 |

| Time | Sound | Edit instruction |

## 3. Dialogue recording / 对白录制

- Record dialogue separately where possible, even if generated clips contain temporary speech.
- Use restrained volume and natural pacing.
- Peter's third denial is urgent but not shouted.
- Keep the rooster on a separate track for frame-accurate overlap.
- If lip-sync is unreliable, use reaction shots and post-recorded dialogue strategically.

- 尽可能单独录制对白，即使生成片段中带有临时对白。
- 音量与语速自然克制。
- 彼得第三次否认应急切，但不是大喊。
- 鸡鸣单独放在一条音轨上，以便精确调整重叠位置。
- 若口型同步不可靠，合理使用反应镜头和后期配音。

## 4. Subtitle and scripture policy / 字幕与经文规范

Dialogue subtitles are faithful subtitles of the film's adapted spoken dialogue, not Bible quotations. Do not add quotation marks or verse numbers to each line. The end card identifies Luke 22:62 only. If a published Bible translation is selected later, maintain a separate scripture subtitle track and credit the edition.

对白字幕忠实呈现影片改编对白，不冒充圣经逐字引用。不添加引号，也不在每句对白后标注经节。片尾只标注路加福音22:62。若之后选择正式圣经译本，应另设经文字幕轨并注明译本。

## 5. End card / 片尾卡

- Black background, centered restrained typography.
- Chinese: 路加福音 22:62
- English: Luke 22:62
- Hold 2–3 seconds.
- No verse wording until the Chinese Bible edition is selected.
- No moralizing tagline.

- 黑色背景，居中克制字体。
- 中文：路加福音 22:62
- 英文：Luke 22:62
- 停留2–3秒。
- 未确定中文圣经译本前，不显示经文逐字译文。
- 不添加说教式标语。

## 6. Delivery specification / 交付规格

**Master recommendation / 主母版建议**
- Picture: 3840×2160 (4K UHD), 16:9 progressive.
- Frame rate: set one project-wide rate before generation/edit; recommend 24 fps. Conform mixed rates before editing.
- Runtime: 90-second story cut; end card may add 2–3 seconds.
- Audio: 48 kHz, 24-bit PCM WAV master; stereo final mix.
- Video: ProRes 422 HQ or equivalent mezzanine master plus H.264/H.265 review copy.
- Color: Rec.709 SDR unless delivery platform requires otherwise.
- Subtitles: separate SRT files and a burned-in review copy; keep Chinese and English separate.
- Archive: generated originals, approved references, prompts, edit project, audio stems, subtitles, final master and checksum manifest.

**主母版建议**
- 画面：3840×2160（4K UHD），16:9逐行扫描。
- 帧率：生成与剪辑前统一项目帧率，建议24fps；不同帧率素材先统一转换。
- 时长：90秒故事正片；片尾卡可额外增加2–3秒。
- 音频：48kHz、24-bit PCM WAV母版；最终立体声混音。
- 视频：ProRes 422 HQ或同等级中间母版，并提供H.264/H.265审阅版。
- 色彩：默认Rec.709 SDR，除非交付平台另有要求。
- 字幕：单独提供SRT及硬字幕审阅版；中英文分开保存。
- 归档：原始生成素材、批准参考图、提示词、剪辑工程、音轨分轨、字幕、最终母版和校验清单。

## 7. Final quality checklist / 最终质量检查

- [ ] Dialogue matches approved screenplay.
- [ ] Adapted lines are not mislabeled as verbatim Bible quotations.
- [ ] Rooster overlaps Peter's final denial.
- [ ] Jesus' turn and Peter's eyeline match geographically.
- [ ] Actual eye contact holds for at least 4 seconds.
- [ ] Peter recognizes before visible grief.
- [ ] No identity, costume, location or screen-direction drift.
- [ ] No modern objects, bags, logos, text artifacts or extra limbs.
- [ ] Dialogue is intelligible on phone speakers.
- [ ] Rooster sounds natural, not comic or painfully loud.
- [ ] Scene works without music.
- [ ] End card cites Luke 22:62 without implying an unselected translation.
- [ ] Master and subtitle files pass full playback review.

- [ ] 对白符合已批准剧本。
- [ ] 不把改编对白误标为圣经逐字引用。
- [ ] 鸡鸣与彼得最后一次否认重叠。
- [ ] 耶稣转身与彼得视线在空间上匹配。
- [ ] 实际对视至少保持4秒。
- [ ] 彼得先意识到，再出现明显悲痛。
- [ ] 不出现人物身份、服装、场景或屏幕方向漂移。
- [ ] 不出现现代物件、包袋、标志、文字瑕疵或多余肢体。
- [ ] 手机扬声器播放时对白仍清晰。
- [ ] 鸡鸣自然，不滑稽、不刺耳。
- [ ] 无音乐时场景依然成立。
- [ ] 片尾引用路加福音22:62，但不暗示采用了尚未选择的译本。
- [ ] 母版与字幕文件通过完整播放检查。
