# The Look (那一眼)

**Status / 状态:** Development — screenplay draft 0.3; storyboard draft 0.2 / 开发中——剧本 Draft 0.3；分镜 Draft 0.2  
**Core passage / 核心经文:** Luke 22:31–34, 54–62 / 路加福音 22:31–34、54–62  
**Format / 画幅:** 16:9 cinematic short film / 16:9 横向电影短片  
**Target runtime / 目标时长:** approximately 90 seconds / 约 90 秒

## Dramatic center / 戏剧核心

Peter sincerely declares that he is ready to follow Jesus even to prison and death. Later, in the courtyard, fear overtakes him and he denies knowing Jesus three times. The rooster crows. Jesus turns and looks at Peter. Peter remembers the prediction and leaves weeping bitterly.

彼得真诚地宣告，自己已经准备好与耶稣一同下监，甚至一同赴死。后来在院子里，恐惧压倒了他，他三次否认认识耶稣。鸡叫了，耶稣转过身来看彼得。彼得想起耶稣的话，出去痛哭。

The emotional climax is the silent exchange of looks, not the third denial by itself.

情绪高潮不是第三次否认本身，而是耶稣与彼得之间那段无声的对视。

## Current creative decisions / 当前创作决定

- **Primary Gospel account / 主要福音书依据:** Luke. Other Gospel accounts are comparison references only unless a deliberate harmonization is approved. / 以路加福音为叙事主线。除非明确批准采用福音书和谐处理，否则其他福音书仅作比较参考。
- **Opening declaration / 开场宣告:** Luke 22:33; do not substitute Matthew's distinctive wording and cite it as Luke. / 依据路加福音 22:33；不得把马太福音特有的措辞替换进来，再标注为路加原文。
- **Dialogue / 对白:** current screenplay lines are adapted paraphrases. No Chinese Bible translation has been selected as the sole authority; direct quotations must be checked against the Greek and identified translation policy. / 当前剧本对白为经文基础上的改写意译。尚未指定某一中文译本为唯一依据；直接引用必须核对希腊文，并遵守明确的译本政策。
- **Rooster / 鸡鸣:** heard off-screen; no need to cut to a rooster. The crow overlaps Peter's third denial, as Luke 22:60 says he was still speaking. / 鸡鸣采用画外音，无须切出公鸡画面。按照路加福音 22:60，鸡叫时彼得仍在说话，因此声音应与第三次否认重叠。
- **The look / 那一眼:** quiet, direct, held long enough to register; no halo, flash, supernatural glow, or slow-motion effect. / 安静、直接，并给予观众足够时间感受；不使用光环、闪白、超自然光效或慢动作。
- **Peter's grief / 彼得的悲痛:** recognition precedes tears; he tries to leave with composure before breaking down. / 先意识到自己的失败，再出现眼泪；他试图维持体面离开，之后才崩溃。
- **Textual discipline / 经文边界:** distinguish what Luke explicitly says, what the production interprets, and what the film invents for staging. / 严格区分路加明确记载的内容、制作团队的理解，以及电影为调度而创作的内容。
- **Production / 制作状态:** no final images or video shots have been generated or approved. / 尚未生成或批准任何正式图片与视频镜头。
