# The Look (那一眼)

**Status:** Development / draft  
**Core passage:** Luke 22:54–62  
**Format:** 16:9 cinematic short film  
**Initial target runtime:** approximately 90 seconds (subject to revision)

## Dramatic center

Peter has sincerely declared his willingness to follow Jesus, but fear overtakes him in the courtyard. After the third denial, the rooster crows. Jesus turns and looks at Peter. Peter remembers Jesus' words and goes out weeping bitterly.

The emotional climax is the silent exchange of looks. Do not reduce the scene to a simple sequence of denials followed by crying.

## Current creative priorities

- Build the story primarily around Luke 22:54–62 so the turn-and-look moment remains grounded in the selected account.
- Verify every direct quotation against the chosen Bible translation.
- Do not combine wording from different Gospel accounts and present it as one verbatim quotation.
- Let Peter's emotional collapse develop through recognition, memory, eye contact, and silence.
- Keep Jesus' expression restrained and compassionate rather than theatrically angry.
- Maintain consistent character identity, courtyard geography, firelight, costume, and screen direction.
