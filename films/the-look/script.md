# The Look — Script

**Status:** Draft not yet finalized  
**Primary source:** Luke 22:54–62  
**Bible translation:** To be selected and recorded before dialogue lock.

## Adaptation note

The scene should follow Luke's account as its primary narrative source. Luke 22:33 records Peter saying that he is ready to go with Jesus both to prison and to death; Luke 22:34 records Jesus' prediction of Peter's denial before the rooster crows. Do not insert wording from another Gospel account and label it as a direct quotation from Luke.

## Dramatic beats

1. **The declaration** — Peter speaks with sincere conviction.
2. **The first denial** — caught off guard, Peter distances himself from Jesus.
3. **The second denial** — pressure increases; his answer becomes more defensive.
4. **The third denial** — fear takes over; he denies urgently.
5. **The rooster** — the sound cuts through the courtyard and breaks the moment.
6. **The look** — Jesus turns and looks at Peter.
7. **Recognition and grief** — Peter remembers the words and leaves, weeping.
