# The Look — Character Design Brief

**Status:** Proposal — Draft 0.1, no character appearance approved  
**Purpose:** Define a consistent, historically grounded visual direction before generating character references.  
**Visual format:** 16:9 horizontal film; photorealistic historical drama.

## 1. Design principles

- Treat Jesus and Peter as first-century Jewish men from Galilee/Judea, not as modern Western fantasy archetypes.
- Favor plausible regional features, natural skin texture, practical hair and beard grooming, and ordinary human proportions.
- Clothing should be simple, layered, and made from plausible natural materials such as wool and linen.
- Avoid polished costume-drama luxury, decorative armor, fantasy robes, modern tailoring, synthetic-looking fabric, and ornamental accessories without a story reason.
- Faces must remain recognizable across the upper room and courtyard.
- Distinguish characters through silhouette, age, posture, grooming, and fabric tone rather than excessive costume decoration.
- No halos, glowing eyes, supernatural visual effects, or beauty-retouched skin.
- Do not add bags or modern carried accessories.

## 2. Peter — proposed direction

### Dramatic identity

Peter should look like an ordinary working man, not a warrior or a heroic statue. His face should carry sincerity and emotional openness. The actor must be able to move from conviction to fear without a change of identity or exaggerated facial styling.

### Tentative visual direction

- Adult Galilean Jewish man; exact age remains open, with a working range of late 30s to mid-40s as a casting/design proposal, not a biblical fact.
- Sun-exposed, weathered skin and a practical working build.
- Dark, naturally textured hair; beard kept ordinary and slightly uneven rather than sculpted.
- Clothing: plain undyed or muted earth-tone tunic, simple outer layer, practical belt and sandals.
- No distinctive jewelry or decorative props.
- A slightly worn, lived-in appearance; not filthy or caricatured.

### Performance and silhouette

- At the supper: open posture, leaning forward, direct gaze, sincere confidence.
- At the courtyard: shoulders gradually draw inward; hands remain occupied or close to the fire naturally; avoid repetitive nervous gestures.
- At the look: recognition should register in the eyes and the stillness of the face before tears.

## 3. Jesus — proposed direction

### Dramatic identity

Jesus should be visually grounded and recognizably human. His authority should come from presence and stillness, not from costume, physical perfection, or supernatural effects.

### Tentative visual direction

- Adult first-century Jewish man from Galilee; exact age remains open, with a working range of early-to-late 30s as a casting/design proposal, not a biblical fact.
- Middle Eastern appearance consistent with the region and period; natural skin texture and ordinary human proportions.
- Dark hair and beard, practical and unstyled.
- Clothing: simple undyed or muted light earth-tone tunic with a plain outer garment; fabric should look woven and worn through use.
- Avoid the polished white robe / European devotional-painting shorthand.
- In the courtyard, show custody through restrained physical handling and posture; do not add graphic injuries or blood unless separately justified and approved.

### Performance and silhouette

- At the supper: calm, attentive, direct; no theatrical prophetic gestures.
- In the courtyard: restrained under custody; he turns toward Peter without exaggerated head movement or visible supernatural cue.
- The look must remain emotionally open to interpretation; do not force a single expression such as anger, disappointment, or pity.

## 4. Servant woman — proposed direction

- Adult woman in ordinary household clothing suitable to a first-century Judean residence.
- Practical, modest, layered garment in muted natural dyes.
- Her role is not villainous. Her recognition is attentive and matter-of-fact.
- Avoid exaggerated suspicion, theatrical pointing, or a caricatured "accuser" expression.

## 5. Two male accusers — proposed direction

The two men should be visually distinct enough to remain identifiable between shots, but neither should look like a villain.

- **First man:** ordinary adult male, plain earth-tone clothing, casual observation; he speaks after the servant woman's accusation.
- **Second man:** a different face and silhouette, ordinary adult male, slightly older or more self-assured as a casting option; his statement is firmer, not aggressive.
- Do not use identical faces, repeated wardrobe, or exaggerated threatening behavior.

## 6. Cross-scene continuity

### Peter and Jesus

- The same face, hair, beard, clothing, and body proportions must be used in both locations.
- No wardrobe change between upper room and courtyard unless the story later establishes a reason.
- Avoid generating each shot independently without reference images and a locked identity sheet.

### Supporting roles

- Servant woman and both men need separate reference images before generating their coverage.
- Keep their clothing and hair consistent across the three denial beats.
- Crowd members may remain background silhouettes unless a shot requires a recognizable face.

## 7. Reference-sheet package

Each approved principal character sheet should include:
- Front portrait, three-quarter portrait, and profile.
- Full-body front and side views.
- Neutral expression and key performance expression.
- Fabric, garment layers, and color palette.
- Hair and beard shape.
- A short identity description that can be reused in every image/video prompt.

Do not generate a multi-angle sheet until the face and overall direction are approved. Do not use a generated reference as final casting approval automatically.

## 8. Decisions still open

- [ ] Confirm the proposed age ranges or choose different ranges.
- [ ] Approve the general regional/historical appearance direction.
- [ ] Decide whether Peter's costume should be slightly darker or more visibly worn than Jesus' clothing.
- [ ] Approve the color separation between Peter, Jesus, and background figures.
- [ ] Approve each face before generating multi-angle reference sheets.
- [ ] Confirm whether the supporting men need full character references or can remain background designs.

# 中文对照版 / Chinese Companion

**状态：**提案 Draft 0.1；尚未批准任何人物外貌。  
**目的：**在生成角色参考图前，确立统一、符合历史环境的视觉方向。  
**画面规格：**16:9横向，真人写实历史电影风格。

## 1. 设计原则

- 耶稣与彼得应是第一世纪加利利/犹太地区的犹太男性，不是现代西方奇幻人物模板。
- 面部特征、皮肤质感、头发和胡须应自然，比例符合普通真人。
- 服装简单、分层，以羊毛、亚麻等合理天然材料为主。
- 避免华丽戏服、装饰性盔甲、奇幻长袍、现代剪裁、合成面料质感，以及没有剧情理由的装饰品。
- 楼上房间与院子中的人物必须保持同一身份。
- 通过轮廓、年龄、姿态、发型和布料色调区分人物，不依赖过多装饰。
- 不使用光环、发光眼睛、超自然特效或过度美颜皮肤。
- 不添加包袋或现代随身配件。

## 2. 彼得——暂定方向

### 戏剧身份
彼得应像一个普通劳动者，而不是战士或英雄雕像。他的脸应带有真诚和情绪开放性。演员需要能够从确信自然过渡到恐惧，不依赖夸张造型。

### 暂定视觉方向
- 成年加利利犹太男性；年龄暂定为三十多岁后段至四十多岁中段。这只是选角/设计提案，不是圣经事实。
- 长期日晒的皮肤，略显风霜，身体结实而实用。
- 深色自然卷曲或有纹理的头发；胡须普通、略不整齐，不做精致雕刻式造型。
- 服装：未染色或低饱和大地色长袍、简单外衣、实用腰带与凉鞋。
- 不佩戴醒目的首饰，不拿装饰性道具。
- 有生活磨损感，但不脏污、不脸谱化。

### 表演与轮廓
- 晚餐时：身体开放、微微前倾、目光直接，带着真诚确信。
- 院子里：肩膀逐渐收紧；双手自然靠近火堆或有所依托，避免重复性的紧张小动作。
- 对视时：先从眼神和面部静止中读出意识变化，再出现眼泪。

## 3. 耶稣——暂定方向

### 戏剧身份
耶稣应真实、可感，是一个具体的人。他的权威来自存在感与沉静，而不是服装、完美外貌或超自然效果。

### 暂定视觉方向
- 第一世纪加利利成年犹太男性；年龄暂定三十多岁。这只是设计提案，不是圣经事实。
- 外貌符合当时当地中东地区人群的合理范围，皮肤质感自然，身体比例普通。
- 深色头发与胡须，朴素、不刻意造型。
- 服装：未染色或浅大地色长袍，外搭简单外衣；布料应有真实织物质感与使用痕迹。
- 避免欧洲宗教绘画中那种光洁纯白长袍的刻板形象。
- 在院子里通过克制的身体控制与姿态表现被拘押状态；未经单独批准，不添加夸张伤口或血迹。

### 表演与轮廓
- 晚餐时：平静、专注、直接，不做戏剧化先知手势。
- 院子里：在被押状态下保持克制，转向彼得时不夸张摆头，也不使用超自然提示。
- 那一眼应保留情绪解释空间，不强迫观众只读出愤怒、失望或怜悯中的某一种。

## 4. 女仆——暂定方向

- 成年女性，穿着符合第一世纪犹太住宅环境的普通家居服装。
- 实用、朴素、分层，使用低饱和天然染色。
- 她不是反派；她的认出应专注、平实。
- 避免夸张怀疑、戏剧化指人或脸谱化的“审问者”表情。

## 5. 两名指认者——暂定方向

两名男子必须足够不同，便于镜头间辨认，但都不应像反派。

- **第一名男子：**普通成年男性，朴素大地色服装，起初只是随意观察，在女仆之后开口。
- **第二名男子：**不同的脸和轮廓；可略年长或显得更有把握，但语气坚定而非攻击性。
- 不要使用相同面孔、重复服装或夸张威胁动作。

## 6. 跨场景连续性

### 彼得与耶稣
- 两个场景必须使用同一张脸、发型、胡须、服装与身体比例。
- 除非故事明确说明理由，否则晚餐厅与院子之间不换服装。
- 不要在没有身份参考表的情况下逐镜头独立生成人物。

### 配角
- 女仆和两名男子在生成相关镜头前应有独立参考图。
- 三次否认之间，服装和头发保持一致。
- 群众除非需要可辨认身份，否则可以作为背景轮廓处理。

## 7. 人物参考表应包含

- 正面、四分之三侧面与侧面肖像。
- 正面和侧面全身图。
- 中性表情与关键表演表情。
- 布料、服装层次与色彩方案。
- 头发与胡须形状。
- 可在每条图片/视频提示词中复用的简短身份描述。

脸和整体方向获批前，不生成多角度定妆表。生成的参考图也不自动等于最终选角批准。
