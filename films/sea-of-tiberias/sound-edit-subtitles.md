# Sound, Edit and Subtitles / 声音、剪辑与字幕

**Version / 版本:** Draft 0.1 / 草稿0.1

## Sound progression / 声音推进

**Night fishing:** water against the hull, oars, rope friction, net entering and leaving water, wet wood, breath, distant night ambience. Keep it natural and sparse. Let repeated empty nets communicate futility.

**夜间捕鱼：** 船身拍水、船桨、绳索摩擦、渔网入水和出水、湿木声、呼吸与遥远夜间环境声。保持自然稀疏，让反复拉起空网表现徒劳。

**The catch:** net tension, rope strain, splashes, and fishermen's effort. Avoid magical impacts, choir, or exaggerated cinematic booms.

**捕鱼成功：** 渔网绷紧、绳索受力、水花和渔夫用力。避免魔法式重击、合唱团或夸张电影低音冲击。

**Breakfast:** charcoal crackle, soft wind, small movements of bread and fish, cloth, footsteps on damp ground. Keep eating sounds understated.

**早餐：** 炭火轻响、微风、饼和鱼的小动作、衣料声、潮湿地面的脚步声。进食声保持克制。

**Three questions:** lower broad ambience slightly but retain fire and wind. No heartbeat, ringing tone, or artificial absolute silence. Let the pauses register.

**三次提问：** 略微降低宽泛环境声，但保留炭火与风声。不要心跳、耳鸣或人为制造绝对寂静。让停顿真正落下来。

**Final call:** footsteps and open-air ambience return. Music, if any, should be minimal and subordinate to “Follow me.”

**最后呼召：** 脚步声与开阔环境声重新进入。若使用音乐，应极少，并服从“你跟从我吧”这句话。

## Edit rhythm / 剪辑节奏

- Night fishing: repetition and ellipsis; do not show every cast.
- Dawn: hold on the distant figure before dialogue.
- Catch: faster physical cuts, preserving spatial clarity.
- Peter's jump: one clear action, not multiple slow-motion angles.
- Breakfast: slow down; show food being offered and received.
- Three questions: three distinct beats, not identical mechanical repetitions.
- Final call: simple movement and clean cut; no explanatory epilogue.

- 夜间捕鱼：通过重复和省略表现整夜，不必展示每次撒网。
- 黎明：对白前多停留一会儿，让观众看见远处的人。
- 捕鱼成功：身体动作剪辑稍快，但保持空间清晰。
- 彼得跳海：一个清楚动作即可，不要多个慢动作角度。
- 早餐：放慢节奏，让观众看见食物被递出和接过。
- 三次提问：三个不同节拍，不做机械重复。
- 最后呼召：简单行动后干净切黑，不加解释性尾声。

## Subtitle principles / 字幕原则

Choose and record the Chinese Bible translation before subtitle lock. Mark adapted dialogue internally; do not label it verbatim Scripture. Preserve pauses between the three questions. Do not add explanatory subtitles for the Greek verb variation. Suggested end reference: John 21:1–19 / 约翰福音21:1–19.

字幕锁定前先确定并记录中文圣经译本。内部标记改编对白，不得标注为逐字经文。保留三次提问之间的停顿。不要加入解释希腊文动词差异的字幕。建议片尾经文出处：约翰福音21:1–19 / John 21:1–19。

## Mix and delivery / 混音与交付

Dialogue intelligibility takes priority. Avoid clipping on splashes, rope strain, and fire transients. Check on headphones, laptop speakers, and phone speakers. Deliver dialogue, music, and effects stems where practical. No final mix is approved until reviewed with locked picture.

对白清晰度优先。避免水花、绳索受力和炭火瞬态声音削波。使用耳机、笔记本和手机扬声器检查。在条件允许时交付对白、音乐和音效分轨。最终混音必须与锁定画面一起审阅后才能批准。
