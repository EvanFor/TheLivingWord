# Translation Comparison / 中文译本比较

**Version / 版本:** Draft 0.1  
**Status / 状态:** Review framework; no translation selected / 对照框架；尚未选定译本

## 1. Policy / 原则

The film will not assume that one Chinese Bible translation is automatically definitive. Compare several Chinese editions with the Greek text, especially where wording affects the performance or meaning. Do not reconstruct or quote a copyrighted edition from memory. Record exact edition, publisher, and wording only after direct verification.

本片不预设某一个中文圣经译本天然具有唯一权威。应将多个中文译本与希腊文对照，尤其关注会影响表演或意义的措辞。不得凭记忆拼凑或引用受版权保护的译本。只有直接核实后，才记录具体版本、出版信息和原文措辞。

## 2. Passages requiring comparison / 重点对照经文

| Passage | Wording issue | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| John 21:5 | Jesus' question about having fish/food | Must sound natural as a distant shore call without changing the sense. |
| John 21:6 | “Right side” and the great catch | Keep the instruction concrete and clear. |
| John 21:7 | “It is the Lord” | Recognition must be immediate and concise. |
| John 21:12 | “Come and have breakfast” | Preserve the ordinary warmth of the invitation. |
| John 21:15 | “Do you love me more than these?” | “These” is ambiguous; do not silently resolve it in the subtitle. |
| John 21:15–17 | Feed/tend/shepherd and lambs/sheep | Preserve the threefold structure and meaningful variation without overexplaining. |
| John 21:17 | Peter is grieved | The wording should communicate sorrow at the third question, not anger at Jesus. |
| John 21:18–19 | Peter's future and “Follow me” | Preserve the seriousness without adding graphic or invented detail. |

| 经文 | 对照重点 | 重要性 |
|---|---|---|
| 约翰福音21:5 | 耶稣询问是否有鱼或食物 | 远距离呼喊应自然，不改变原意。 |
| 约翰福音21:6 | “右边”与大量鱼获 | 指示应具体清楚。 |
| 约翰福音21:7 | “是主” | 认出主的台词应直接简洁。 |
| 约翰福音21:12 | “来吃早饭” | 保留邀请中平常而温暖的意味。 |
| 约翰福音21:15 | “你爱我比这些更深吗” | “这些”存在歧义，字幕不要擅自消除。 |
| 约翰福音21:15–17 | 喂养/牧养、小羊/羊 | 保留三重结构和有意义的措辞变化，不要过度解释。 |
| 约翰福音21:17 | 彼得忧愁 | 应表达第三次提问使彼得忧伤，而非他对耶稣发怒。 |
| 约翰福音21:18–19 | 彼得的未来与“跟从我” | 保留严肃性，不增加血腥或虚构细节。 |

## 3. Greek control points / 希腊文核对点

- The phrase translated “more than these” should remain open in the film; the referent is debated.
- The forms of agapaō and phileō vary across the three exchanges. Do not force a rigid theological distinction into the subtitles.
- The three commissions use related but not identical language. Keep the distinction where the selected translation naturally preserves it.
- Peter's grief in the third exchange is explicit in the narrative.

- “比这些更深”这一短语在影片中应保持开放，不要替观众决定“这些”指什么。
- agapaō 与 phileō 在三次问答中的形式有所变化，不要把固定的神学等级强加到字幕里。
- 三次托付的措辞彼此相关但并不完全相同；若选定译本自然保留差异，应尽量保留。
- 第三次问答中彼得忧愁是经文明确记载的。
