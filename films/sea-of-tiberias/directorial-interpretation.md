# Directorial Interpretation / 导演阐释

**Version / 版本:** Draft 0.1  
**Status / 状态:** Proposed direction, not approved / 创作提案，尚未批准

## Central question / 核心问题

What does restoration look like when love has already failed under pressure?

当一个人的爱曾在压力之下失败，恢复会是什么样子？

This is not a story about Peter proving he has become brave. Jesus meets the disciples in their exhaustion, feeds them, addresses Peter truthfully, entrusts him with care, and calls him forward.

这不是一个证明彼得如今已经足够勇敢的故事。耶稣在门徒疲惫时与他们相遇，先喂养他们，再真实地面对彼得，把照顾羊群的责任托付给他，并呼召他继续前行。

## Dramatic progression / 戏剧推进

1. **Labor without result / 徒劳：** a whole night of work produces nothing.
2. **Recognition / 认出：** an ordinary instruction leads to an extraordinary catch; the beloved disciple recognizes Jesus.
3. **Urgency / 急切：** Peter moves toward Jesus before the others reach shore.
4. **Hospitality / 款待：** Jesus has already prepared food; he does not begin with accusation.
5. **Truthful confrontation / 真实面对：** the repeated question reaches the place Peter cannot avoid.
6. **Commission / 托付：** Peter is not merely comforted; he is entrusted with people.
7. **Following / 跟从：** the story ends with a call into the future.

## Breakfast matters / 早餐本身很重要

Do not rush from the catch directly into the questions. The meal gives the scene human ground. The disciples are tired and hungry; Jesus offers bread and fish. The risen Lord is present with them in an ordinary meal.

不要从捕鱼成功直接跳到三问。早餐为整场戏建立真实的人性基础。门徒疲惫而饥饿，耶稣把饼和鱼递给他们。复活的主与他们同在，甚至同他们吃一顿平常的早餐。

The charcoal fire visually echoes John 18:18. No explanatory flashback is needed.

炭火在视觉上呼应约翰福音18:18，不需要解释性闪回。

## Peter's performance arc / 彼得的表演弧线

- Fishing: physically tired, focused on work, not theatrically despairing.
- Recognition: sudden, impulsive urgency; no superhero leap.
- Breakfast: grateful but unsettled; he senses a conversation may come.
- First question: plain answer, with some confidence and affection.
- Second question: repetition begins to trouble him; he searches Jesus' face.
- Third question: grief becomes visible, but he does not collapse into melodrama. He appeals to Jesus' knowledge.
- Commission: he receives responsibility humbly, attentive rather than triumphant.

- 捕鱼时：身体疲惫，专注劳作，不演成戏剧化绝望。
- 认出耶稣时：突然、冲动而急切，不是超级英雄式跳海。
- 早餐时：心怀感激，却隐约不安，感觉之后可能有一场谈话。
- 第一次提问：平实回答，仍带着一些确信与亲近。
- 第二次提问：重复开始让他不安，他寻找耶稣的目光。
- 第三次提问：忧伤显露，但不崩溃式表演；他诉诸耶稣对自己的了解。
- 接受托付时：谦卑承接责任，专注而非得胜式昂首。

## Jesus' performance / 耶稣的表演

Jesus is neither prosecutor nor sentimental comforter. He is calm, present, direct, and compassionate. He does not sneer, dramatize Peter's failure, or use anger to force an answer. The questions are serious; the commission is real.

耶稣既不是审判官，也不是煽情的安慰者。他平静、临在、直接而有怜悯。他不冷笑，不夸大彼得的失败，也不以愤怒逼迫回答。提问是严肃的，托付也是真实的。

## Visual grammar / 视觉语法

- Night fishing: cool blue-gray water, practical lanterns, broad negative space.
- Dawn: natural pale horizon light; no supernatural beam or glowing silhouette.
- Shore meal: limestone, damp ground, smoke, embers, bread and fish; tactile close-ups.
- Three questions: stable camera, measured shot/reverse-shot, intimacy through framing and pauses.
- Final call: open shoreline and simple forward movement; avoid a triumphant sunrise cliché.

- 夜间捕鱼：蓝灰色海水、实用灯火、大面积留白。
- 黎明：自然浅色地平线光线，不要神迹光束或发光剪影。
- 岸边早餐：石灰岩、潮湿地面、烟、余烬、饼和鱼，强调真实质感。
- 三次提问：稳定摄影机，以景别和停顿推进亲密感。
- 最后呼召：开阔海岸与简单的前行动作，避免凯旋式日出陈词滥调。

## Ending / 结尾

The final note is not “Peter has been cleared of guilt.” It is “Peter is called to follow and care for others.” End on the movement implied by “Follow me,” not an explanatory speech or sentimental embrace.

结尾不是“彼得已经被免除罪责”，而是“彼得再次蒙召跟从，并照顾别人”。以“你跟从我吧”所暗示的行动结束，不加解释性演讲或煽情拥抱。
