## Animatic plan / 动态分镜计划

Build the animatic after storyboard timing is reviewed. Use temporary stills, simple camera moves, scratch dialogue, and timing cards. Test:
- Does the fishing feel like a full night rather than a few seconds?
- Is the dawn encounter and recognition clear?
- Do Peter's jump and the boat's arrival preserve John 21:7–8?
- Does the meal register as an act of hospitality?
- Are the three questions distinct and emotionally progressive?
- Does the final call have enough space without an explanatory ending?

分镜时长审阅后再制作动态分镜。使用临时静帧、简单摄影机运动、临时对白和时间卡。检验：
- 捕鱼是否让人感受到整整一夜，而不是短短几秒？
- 黎明相遇与认出主的过程是否清楚？
- 彼得跳海和渔船靠岸是否遵循约翰福音21:7–8？
- 早餐是否成为真实的款待行为？
- 三次提问是否各自清楚并具有情绪递进？
- 最后的呼召是否有足够空间落地，而不需要解释性结尾？

## Approval rule / 资产批准规则

A reference image is not approved merely because it looks attractive. Check historical plausibility, stable identity, anatomy, costume, material quality, composition, and continuity. Keep rejected generations out of the approved reference set.

参考图不能仅因好看就获批。必须检查历史合理性、人物身份稳定性、人体结构、服装、材质、构图和连续性。被否决的生成结果不得进入已批准参考集。

No character image, location image, storyboard image, video shot, voice recording, or animatic is recorded as generated at this stage. Add a dated entry only after the asset exists and has been reviewed.

当前阶段尚未记录任何人物图、场景图、分镜图、视频镜头、配音或动态分镜已生成。只有资产实际存在并经过审阅后，才能添加带日期的记录。
