# Screenplay / 剧本

**Version / 版本:** Draft 0.1  
**Status / 状态:** Narrative draft for review; dialogue is adapted, not a locked Bible quotation / 叙事草稿，待审核；对白为改编，非已锁定译本的逐字引用

## Creative note / 创作说明

The controlling account is John 21:1–19. Dialogue is compressed for a short film and must be checked against the selected Chinese Bible translation before subtitle lock. Exact blocking, pauses, and shoreline geography are cinematic choices.

主叙事依据为约翰福音21:1–19。对白为适应短片而压缩，正式锁定字幕前必须与选定的中文圣经译本核对。具体调度、停顿和海岸空间均属于电影创作决定。

## SCENE 1 — THE EMPTY NET / 第一场：空网
**EXT. SEA OF GALILEE — NIGHT TO DAWN / 外景·加利利海——深夜至黎明**

Black water. A small fishing boat moves through the night. Nets fall, drag, and rise empty. Hands repeat the work. No one speaks much.

黑色水面。一艘小渔船在夜色中移动。渔网撒下、拖起，依旧空空如也。双手重复劳作，没有人多说话。

**PETER:** I'm going fishing.  
**彼得：** 我去打鱼。

The others climb in with him. Hours pass through repetition: rope sliding through wet hands, oars, a net pulled aboard, empty.

其他门徒跟着上船。时间通过重复动作流逝：湿绳从手中滑过，船桨划水，渔网被拉上船，仍然空空如也。

At first light, a man stands on the shore. The disciples do not recognize him.

天刚亮，一个人站在岸边。门徒没有认出他。

**JESUS:** Children, have you caught anything to eat?  
**耶稣：** 孩子们，有鱼吃吗？

**DISCIPLES:** No.  
**门徒：** 没有。

**JESUS:** Cast the net on the right side of the boat, and you will find some.  
**耶稣：** 把网撒在船的右边，就会有收获。

They cast. The net pulls hard. The boat shifts under the sudden weight. Hands strain against the rope.

他们撒网。渔网突然沉重起来。船身随重量偏移，众人用力拉住绳索。

**BELOVED DISCIPLE (quietly):** It is the Lord.  
**所爱的门徒（轻声）：** 是主。

Peter looks toward the shore. He puts on his outer garment and jumps into the water.

彼得望向岸边，披上外衣，跳进水里。

## SCENE 2 — BREAKFAST BY THE FIRE / 第二场：炭火旁的早餐
**EXT. SHORE — DAWN / 外景·海岸——黎明**

The others bring the boat in, dragging the net. On the shore: a charcoal fire, fish laid over it, and bread.

其他门徒把船划到岸边，拖着渔网。岸上已经有一处炭火，火上有鱼，旁边有饼。

Peter reaches the shore, breathing hard. Jesus does not question him. He looks at the group.

彼得上岸，急促喘息。耶稣没有先质问他，而是看向众人。

**JESUS:** Bring some of the fish you have just caught.  
**耶稣：** 把刚才打的鱼拿几条来。

Peter and the others bring the net ashore. The catch fills the frame briefly—not as spectacle, but as the result of a night transformed.

彼得和其他门徒把网拖上岸。鱼获短暂占据画面——不是奇观，而是一整夜劳作被改变的结果。

**JESUS:** Come and have breakfast.  
**耶稣：** 你们来吃早饭。

No one asks who he is. They know. Jesus takes bread and gives it to them, and likewise the fish.

没有人问他是谁。他们都知道。耶稣拿起饼递给他们，也把鱼分给他们。

A quiet meal. The fire crackles. Peter eats, but his eyes occasionally return to Jesus. Jesus does not hurry him.

一顿安静的早餐。炭火轻响。彼得吃着东西，目光偶尔回到耶稣身上。耶稣没有催促他。

## SCENE 3 — DO YOU LOVE ME? / 第三场：你爱我吗？
**EXT. SHORE — MORNING / 外景·海岸——清晨**

The meal is over. The others remain nearby, out of focus. Jesus and Peter sit within sight of the charcoal fire. No one performs for the others.

早餐结束。其他门徒仍在附近，虚化在背景中。耶稣和彼得坐在炭火旁，彼此都看得见火。没有人刻意向其他人表演。

**JESUS:** Simon, son of John, do you love me more than these?  
**耶稣：** 约翰的儿子西门，你爱我比这些更深吗？

**PETER:** Yes, Lord. You know that I love you.  
**彼得：** 主啊，是的，你知道我爱你。

**JESUS:** Feed my lambs.  
**耶稣：** 你喂养我的小羊。

A pause. Jesus asks again.

停顿。耶稣再次提问。

**JESUS:** Simon, son of John, do you love me?  
**耶稣：** 约翰的儿子西门，你爱我吗？

**PETER:** Yes, Lord. You know that I love you.  
**彼得：** 主啊，是的，你知道我爱你。

**JESUS:** Shepherd my sheep.  
**耶稣：** 你牧养我的羊。

Peter looks down, then back at Jesus. The repetition is no longer easy.

彼得低下目光，又看向耶稣。重复的问题已经不再轻松。

A longer silence.

更长的沉默。

**JESUS:** Simon, son of John, do you love me?  
**耶稣：** 约翰的儿子西门，你爱我吗？

Peter is grieved. His answer comes quietly, without defense.

彼得忧愁起来。他不再辩解，只是轻声回答。

**PETER:** Lord, you know everything. You know that I love you.  
**彼得：** 主啊，你知道一切；你知道我爱你。

**JESUS:** Feed my sheep.  
**耶稣：** 你喂养我的羊。

## SCENE 4 — FOLLOW ME / 第四场：跟从我
**EXT. SHORE — MORNING / 外景·海岸——清晨**

Jesus speaks of the road Peter will one day walk. We do not illustrate the death. We stay with Peter listening.

耶稣谈到彼得将来要走的道路。我们不把他的死亡拍成画面，只留下彼得认真聆听的神情。

A silence. Jesus rises and turns toward the open shore.

沉默。耶稣起身，转向开阔的海岸。

**JESUS:** Follow me.  
**耶稣：** 你跟从我吧。

Peter looks at Jesus, then rises and follows. Hold on the two figures moving along the shore. Cut before the image becomes triumphant.

彼得看着耶稣，随后起身跟上。镜头停留在两人沿岸前行的背影上。在画面变成凯旋式场面之前切黑。

**END / 结束**
