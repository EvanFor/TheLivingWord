# Production Bible / 制作总纲

**Version / 版本:** Draft 0.1  
**Status / 状态:** Production proposal; approvals required / 制作提案；需要审核批准  
**Primary text / 核心经文:** John 21:1–19 / 约翰福音21:1–19  
**Frame / 画幅:** 16:9 horizontal / 16:9横向  
**Runtime / 时长:** 170-second storyboard proposal / 分镜暂定170秒

## Film identity / 影片定位

**Logline:** After a fruitless night on the Sea of Galilee, the disciples recognize the risen Jesus on the shore. He feeds them beside a charcoal fire, then asks Peter three times whether he loves him—and calls him to follow.

**一句话梗概：** 在加利利海上徒劳捕鱼一夜之后，门徒在岸边认出复活的耶稣。耶稣在炭火旁喂养他们，随后三次询问彼得是否爱他，并再次呼召他跟从。

**Dramatic center / 戏剧核心:** Restoration is not merely the removal of shame. Peter is met, fed, questioned, entrusted, and called forward.

恢复不只是羞愧被抹去。彼得被接纳、被喂养、被询问、被托付，并被呼召继续前行。

## Canonical boundaries / 经文边界

- John 21:1–19 is the controlling narrative. Do not silently harmonize details from other Gospels.
- The meal happens before the three questions.
- Jesus addresses Peter as Simon son of John.
- The threefold question and threefold commission remain intact.
- The charcoal fire echoes John 18:18, but the text does not explain it as a deliberate memory exercise.
- Do not invent a definitive symbolic meaning for the 153 fish or a rigid hierarchy between the Greek love verbs.

- 约翰福音21:1–19是唯一主叙事依据，不得悄悄混入其他福音书的细节。
- 早餐发生在三次问答之前。
- 耶稣称彼得为约翰的儿子西门。
- 三重提问和三重托付必须完整保留。
- 炭火呼应约翰福音18:18，但经文没有解释这是一次刻意的记忆训练。
- 不得虚构153条鱼的确定象征意义，也不得把希腊文两个“爱”动词划分为固定等级。

## Visual direction / 视觉方向

- Night: cool blue-gray water, practical lanterns, broad negative space.
- Dawn: natural pale light gradually reveals the shore; no supernatural beam, halo, glowing figure, or artificial flare.
- Boat: weathered wood, rope, coarse net, believable weight and water behavior.
- Shore: damp stones, compacted earth, modest vegetation, charcoal fire, simple fish and bread.
- Breakfast: tactile close-ups of hands, bread, fish, embers, and quiet faces.
- Three questions: stable camera, measured shot/reverse-shot, increasing intimacy through framing and pauses.
- Final call: open shoreline and simple forward movement.

- 夜晚：蓝灰色海水、实用灯火、大面积留白。
- 黎明：自然浅色晨光逐渐显露海岸；不要神迹光束、光环、发光人物或人为眩光。
- 渔船：风化木材、绳索、粗织渔网，重量与水的运动必须可信。
- 海岸：潮湿石块、压实泥土、少量植被、炭火、简单的鱼和饼。
- 早餐：强调手、饼、鱼、余烬和安静面孔的真实质感。
- 三次提问：摄影机稳定，以景别和停顿增加亲密感。
- 最后呼召：开阔海岸与简单的前行动作。

## Performance / 表演

Jesus is calm, attentive, direct, and compassionate; no scolding or theatrical solemnity. Peter is exhausted and impulsive while fishing, grateful but unsettled at breakfast, increasingly grieved during the repeated questions, and humble when entrusted. Other disciples remain naturally present.

耶稣平静、专注、直接而有怜悯，不训斥、不刻意摆出庄严姿态。彼得捕鱼时疲惫而冲动，早餐时感激却不安，随着重复提问逐渐忧伤，接受托付时谦卑。其他门徒自然地在场。

## Sound / 声音

The first movement is carried by water, rope, wood, breath, and wind. The catch is heard through the net tightening and bodies straining, not a musical hit. Breakfast is quiet and close. During the questions, reduce ambience but retain fire and wind. Music, if any, is minimal.

第一段由水声、绳索、木头、呼吸和风声推动。捕鱼成功通过渔网绷紧和身体用力表现，而不是靠音乐重击。早餐安静而贴近。三次问答时降低环境声，但保留炭火与风声。若使用音乐，应极少。

## Continuity priorities / 连戏重点

1. Same boat, net, clothing, wetness, and fatigue across night and dawn.
2. Peter's outer garment is handled consistently when he jumps into the water and reaches shore.
3. The catch remains in the net until brought ashore.
4. Fire, fish, bread, seating, and smoke direction remain consistent during breakfast.
5. Jesus and Peter's screen direction and eyelines remain consistent through all three questions.
6. Morning light direction and intensity remain consistent.
7. No flashback to the earlier denial unless explicitly approved.

1. 夜晚到黎明的渔船、渔网、服装、湿润程度和疲惫状态保持连续。
2. 彼得跳海及上岸时外衣的处理保持一致。
3. 鱼获在上岸前始终留在渔网中。
4. 早餐期间炭火、鱼、饼、座位和烟气方向保持一致。
5. 三次问答中耶稣与彼得的屏幕方向和视线保持一致。
6. 晨光方向和强度保持一致。
7. 未经明确批准，不插入彼得此前否认主的闪回。

## Technical baseline / 技术基线

16:9 horizontal; photorealistic live-action historical drama; natural skin and fabric texture; cool night tones transitioning to restrained dawn earth tones; warm fire as a localized accent. Generate approved character and location references first, then individual shots. No shot is approved until identity, anatomy, props, geography, lighting, and motion pass review.

16:9横向；真人写实历史剧情片；自然皮肤与织物纹理；夜晚冷色逐渐过渡到克制的黎明大地色；暖色炭火只作为局部视觉锚点。先建立并批准人物和场景参考，再逐镜头生成。镜头只有在人物身份、人体结构、道具、空间、光线和动态均通过审查后才可批准。
