# The Sea of Tiberias — 提比哩亚海边

**Status / 状态:** Development — Draft 0.1; nothing approved / 开发中——Draft 0.1；尚无内容获批  
**Primary passage / 核心经文:** John 21:1–19 / 约翰福音21:1–19  
**Format / 规格:** 16:9 horizontal live-action cinematic short / 16:9横向真人电影短片  
**Target runtime / 目标时长:** approximately 170 seconds, subject to animatic review / 约170秒，待动态分镜审查

## Dramatic center / 戏剧核心

After a fruitless night of fishing, the disciples encounter the risen Jesus on the shore. He feeds them before asking Peter three times whether he loves him. The scene moves from exhaustion and recognition to nourishment, grief, restoration, and renewed responsibility.

门徒整夜劳碌却一无所获，直到在岸边遇见复活的耶稣。耶稣先为他们预备早餐，然后三次询问彼得是否爱他。故事从疲惫与认出主，走向饱足、忧伤、恢复与重新承担使命。

The film must not reduce the passage to a sentimental apology scene. The breakfast matters: Jesus meets the disciples' physical need before the difficult conversation. The charcoal fire deliberately echoes Peter's denial in John 18:18, while remaining a new scene with its own meaning.

影片不能把这段经文简化成一场煽情的道歉戏。早餐本身很重要：在艰难的谈话开始前，耶稣先回应门徒真实的身体需要。炭火有意呼应约翰福音18:18中彼得否认主的场景，但它仍是一个具有自身意义的新场景。
