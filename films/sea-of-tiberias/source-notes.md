## Source discipline / 经文研究原则

Keep textual observation, interpretation, and cinematic invention separate. A film decision must never be presented as a detail explicitly stated by Scripture.

必须区分经文观察、解释和电影创作。电影创作决定不得冒充圣经明确记载的事实。

## Passage map / 经文段落索引

| Passage | Textual observation | Film relevance |
|---|---|---|
| John 21:1–3 | Seven disciples are present. Peter says he is going fishing; others join him. They catch nothing during the night. | Open with ordinary fishing, fatigue, darkness, and an empty net. |
| John 21:4–6 | At daybreak Jesus stands on shore, unrecognized. He asks whether they have fish; they answer no. At his instruction they cast on the right side and catch a great quantity. | Keep the stranger's identity visually concealed until recognition. Show weight and effort, not fantasy effects. |
| John 21:7–8 | The beloved disciple recognizes Jesus. Peter puts on his outer garment and jumps into the sea. The others approach by boat, dragging the net, about two hundred cubits from shore. | Preserve the sequence and credible geography. |
| John 21:9–14 | A charcoal fire, fish, and bread are already on shore. Jesus invites them to eat and gives them bread and fish. | Breakfast is a central act of hospitality, not a disposable transition. |
| John 21:15–17 | After breakfast Jesus asks Peter three times whether he loves him. Each answer is followed by a charge concerning the lambs or sheep. Peter is grieved at the third question. | Preserve three distinct exchanges and their emotional progression. |
| John 21:18–19 | Jesus speaks of Peter's future and the kind of death by which he would glorify God, then says, “Follow me.” | End with the call, not a graphic depiction of death. |

## Details to preserve / 必须保留的细节

- The disciples fish through the night and catch nothing; the great catch follows Jesus' instruction.
- John gives the number as 153 fish and says the net was not torn. Do not invent a definitive symbolic explanation for the number.
- Jesus addresses Peter as “Simon son of John” in the three questions.
- The meal precedes the questions.
- The threefold question and threefold commission must remain intact.
- The exact shoreline, boat design, weather, fish species, character ages, and duration of breakfast are not specified.

- 门徒整夜捕鱼却一无所获；大量鱼获发生在听从耶稣指示之后。
- 约翰记载鱼有153条，并说网没有破。不得虚构这个数字的确定象征含义。
- 三次问答中，耶稣称彼得为“约翰的儿子西门”。
- 早餐发生在三次提问之前。
- 三重提问和三重托付必须完整保留。
- 具体海岸、船只样式、天气、鱼种、人物年龄和早餐时长均未由经文说明。

## The two love verbs / 两个“爱”动词

Jesus' first two questions use forms of agapaō (ἀγαπάω); Peter's first two answers use forms of phileō (φιλέω). In the third exchange Jesus uses phileō, and Peter also answers with phileō. This variation is real, but it does not prove a rigid hierarchy in which one verb always means divine unconditional love and the other merely friendship. Their semantic ranges overlap in John, and interpreters differ on how much weight to assign to the variation.

耶稣前两次提问使用 agapaō（ἀγαπάω）的形式，彼得前两次回答使用 phileō（φιλέω）的形式。第三次问答中，耶稣和彼得都使用 phileō。这种差异确实存在，但不足以证明一个词永远代表神圣无条件的爱，另一个词仅代表友谊。两者在约翰福音中的语义范围存在重叠，解释者对这一区别应承载多少分量也有不同看法。

Therefore, do not stage the third question as Jesus lowering the standard or accepting a lesser love as a settled fact. Let the emotional movement come from repetition, Peter's grief, and the commission.

因此，不要把第三次提问演成耶稣降低标准、接受较低层次的爱，并将其当作定论。情绪递进应来自问题的重复、彼得的忧伤和随后的托付。

## Charcoal-fire echo / 炭火呼应

John 18:18 describes Peter warming himself by a charcoal fire during his denial. John 21:9 mentions a charcoal fire on the shore. The same Greek noun, anthrakia (ἀνθρακιά), appears in both passages. This is a strong literary echo. The text does not explicitly say Jesus built the fire to recreate Peter's memory or staged the conversation as punishment. Do not add a flashback by default; allow the audience to recognize the echo.

约翰福音18:18记载彼得在炭火旁取暖并否认耶稣；21:9记载岸边另一处炭火。两处使用同一个希腊名词 anthrakia（ἀνθρακιά），构成有力的文学呼应。但经文没有明确说耶稣特意生火重现彼得的记忆，也没有说这场谈话是一种惩罚。默认不加入闪回，让观众自行认出这层呼应。
