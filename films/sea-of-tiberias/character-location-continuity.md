# Character, Location and Continuity / 人物、场景与连戏

**Version / 版本:** Draft 0.1  
**Status / 状态:** Visual proposal only; no reference image approved / 仅为视觉提案；尚无参考图获批

## Characters / 人物

### Jesus / 耶稣
Adult Jewish man of the first century; historically grounded natural appearance; simple woven tunic and outer garment in muted undyed or earth-toned fabric. Calm, attentive posture. No halo, glowing eyes, fantasy styling, or visual effects. Preserve exact face, hair, beard, apparent age, costume, and proportions across shots.

第一世纪成年犹太男性，外貌自然并符合历史语境；穿着朴素织物长袍和低饱和未染色或大地色外衣。姿态平静专注。不要光环、发光眼睛、奇幻造型或视觉特效。所有镜头保持面孔、头发、胡须、年龄观感、服装和身体比例一致。

### Simon Peter / 西门彼得
Adult fisherman, physically capable but visibly tired after a night of work. Weathered hands, practical first-century clothing, damp fabric after entering the water. Emotion is complex but restrained. Track the outer garment before and after the jump.

成年渔夫，身体健壮但经过整夜劳作后明显疲惫。双手有劳作痕迹，穿着符合时代的实用服装，入水后布料潮湿。情绪复杂但克制。追踪跳海前后外衣的状态。

### Beloved disciple / 所爱的门徒
Adult male disciple, understated and observant. He recognizes Jesus first in the boat sequence. Do not identify him by name unless the script explicitly chooses to.

成年男性门徒，表演克制、善于观察。在船上段落中先认出耶稣。除非剧本明确决定，否则不必给他标注姓名。

### Other disciples / 其他门徒
The passage names or counts a group of seven. Show a group consistent with the text without inventing individual backstories or giving every person a speaking line.

经文提及七位门徒。画面可呈现与经文相符的小组，不虚构每个人的背景故事，也不必让每个人都有台词。

## Locations / 场景

### Sea and boat / 海面与渔船
A modest first-century fishing boat, weathered wood, believable scale and buoyancy. Coarse net, rope, and oars; no modern hardware or synthetic materials. Boat orientation relative to shore must remain fixed.

朴素的第一世纪渔船，木材有使用痕迹，尺寸与浮力可信。使用粗织渔网、绳索和船桨，不出现现代五金件或合成材料。渔船与海岸的相对方向必须固定。

### Shore / 海岸
A plausible Galilean lakeshore, not a specific archaeological reconstruction: wet stones, compacted earth, modest vegetation, open water and distant shoreline. No modern docks, tourist facilities, or contemporary buildings. “Sea of Tiberias” is John's name for the Sea of Galilee; do not label the setting as the modern city of Tiberias.

符合加利利地区环境的湖岸，但不冒充具体考古遗址复原：潮湿石块、压实泥土、少量植被、开阔水面和远处岸线。避免现代码头、旅游设施和当代建筑。“提比哩亚海”是约翰对加利利海的称呼，不要把场景标注为现代提比哩亚城。

### Charcoal fire and breakfast / 炭火与早餐
A small practical charcoal fire with visible embers and natural smoke. Simple fish and bread; no elaborate feast. Fix the positions of fire, Jesus, Peter, food, and other disciples. The fire echoes John 18:18; no flashback by default.

小型真实炭火，可见余烬与自然烟气。食物为简单的鱼和饼，不出现华丽宴席。固定炭火、耶稣、彼得、食物和其他门徒的位置。炭火呼应约翰福音18:18，默认不添加闪回。

## Screen geography / 屏幕空间

Approve an overhead layout before final generation: boat approach and landing point; fire location and distance from water; net landing position; Jesus and Peter seating positions; other disciples' background positions; morning light and wind direction.

正式生成前批准俯视布局：渔船靠岸方向与上岸点；炭火位置及其与水边的距离；渔网上岸位置；耶稣与彼得的座位；其他门徒的背景位置；晨光与风向。

## Continuity checklist / 连戏检查

- [ ] Same character identity, face, hair, beard, age impression, and proportions.
- [ ] Same costume layers and garment wetness state.
- [ ] Same boat, net, rope, oars, and catch impression.
- [ ] No fish teleportation or unexplained net changes.
- [ ] Same shoreline geography and boat landing direction.
- [ ] Same fire, food placement, and smoke direction.
- [ ] Consistent morning light and shadow direction.
- [ ] Consistent eyelines and screen direction through all three questions.
- [ ] Performance progression matches the script.
- [ ] No modern objects, fantasy elements, or anachronisms.

- [ ] 人物身份、面孔、头发、胡须、年龄观感和身体比例一致。
- [ ] 服装层次及湿润状态一致。
- [ ] 渔船、渔网、绳索、船桨和鱼获观感一致。
- [ ] 鱼不得凭空出现，渔网不得无故变化。
- [ ] 海岸空间与渔船靠岸方向一致。
- [ ] 炭火、食物位置和烟气方向一致。
- [ ] 晨光与阴影方向一致。
- [ ] 三次问答的视线与屏幕方向一致。
- [ ] 表演递进符合剧本。
- [ ] 不出现现代物件、奇幻元素或时代错置。
