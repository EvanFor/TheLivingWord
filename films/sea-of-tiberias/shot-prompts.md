## Global visual header / 全局视觉提示词

**EN:** Photorealistic live-action historical drama set on the Sea of Galilee in the first century. Natural human anatomy, realistic skin texture, historically plausible woven linen and wool garments, weathered wooden fishing boat, coarse fishing net and rope, practical charcoal fire, restrained cinematic realism, natural dawn light, tactile materials, subtle atmospheric depth, 16:9 horizontal composition. Preserve exact approved character identities, faces, apparent ages, hair, beards, costumes, and body proportions. Preserve approved shoreline geography, boat orientation, fire placement, screen direction, and eyelines. Understated human performance; physically plausible water and cloth movement.

**中文：** 第一世纪加利利海背景的真人写实历史剧情片。自然人体结构、真实皮肤质感、符合时代的亚麻与羊毛织物、风化木制渔船、粗织渔网与绳索、真实炭火、克制的电影写实风格、自然黎明光线、可触摸的材质和细腻空间层次，16:9横向构图。严格保持已批准人物参考中的身份、面孔、年龄观感、头发、胡须、服装和身体比例。严格保持已批准的海岸地理、船只方向、炭火位置、屏幕方向和视线。人物表演自然克制，水和布料运动符合物理规律。

## Global negative prompt / 全局排除项

No halo, glowing eyes, supernatural beams, fantasy armor, medieval European styling, modern boats, engines, plastic, synthetic netting, modern docks, tourist facilities, modern tableware, ornate feast, modern footwear, bags or backpacks, extra fingers, distorted hands, duplicated faces, identity drift, plastic skin, artificial orange wash, excessive lens flare, oversaturated colors, painterly or cartoon appearance, impossible water physics.

不要光环、发光眼睛、神迹光束、奇幻盔甲、中世纪欧洲风格、现代船只、发动机、塑料、合成渔网、现代码头、旅游设施、现代餐具、华丽宴席、现代鞋履、包或背包、多余手指、扭曲的手、重复面孔、人物身份漂移、塑料皮肤、人工橙色滤镜、过度镜头光晕、过饱和色彩、绘画或卡通外观、不符合物理规律的水体运动。

## Sequence prompts / 分段提示词

### Night fishing / 夜间捕鱼
**EN:** Wide cinematic view of a small first-century wooden fishing boat on dark open water at night, adult fishermen working a coarse net by low practical lantern light, repeated labor, wet rope and wood, restrained blue-gray palette, large negative space, realistic water movement, quiet exhaustion, no modern shoreline structures.

**中文：** 夜晚开阔水面上的小型第一世纪木制渔船电影广角镜头，成年渔夫在微弱实用灯火下操作粗织渔网，重复劳作，绳索和木材潮湿，克制蓝灰色调，大面积留白，真实水面运动，安静的疲惫感，不出现现代海岸建筑。

### Dawn figure / 黎明岸上人物
**EN:** From the boat, a distant adult man stands quietly on a natural Galilean lakeshore at first light, face not yet identifiable, pale dawn horizon, still water, understated silhouette, no supernatural glow.

**中文：** 从船上望向岸边，一名成年男子安静站在自然加利利湖岸，天刚亮，面孔尚不可辨认，地平线泛起浅色晨光，水面平静，剪影克制，不带神迹发光效果。

### Heavy net / 沉重渔网
**EN:** Coarse fishing net suddenly pulls taut in water; adult fishermen strain with believable weight, wet rope under tension, wooden boat shifts subtly, natural splash and water resistance, no giant-fish spectacle.

**中文：** 粗织渔网在水中突然绷紧，成年渔夫承受真实重量，湿绳受力，木船轻微偏移，水花与阻力自然，不表现巨鱼奇观。

### Peter enters water / 彼得跳海
**EN:** Adult first-century fisherman reacts urgently after recognizing the man on shore, secures his outer garment and enters the water with a physically plausible forward jump, natural splash, no superhero pose, preserve approved Peter identity.

**中文：** 成年第一世纪渔夫认出岸上的人后急切反应，整理外衣，以符合物理规律的动作向前跳入水中，水花自然，不摆超级英雄姿势，严格保持已批准的彼得形象。

### Charcoal breakfast / 炭火早餐
**EN:** Quiet shoreline breakfast at dawn, small charcoal fire with visible embers, simple fish and bread, Jesus calmly offering food to tired adult disciples, weathered hands, natural smoke, damp stones and wood, intimate observational framing, warm fire as a small accent against cool morning tones.

**中文：** 黎明海岸边安静的早餐，小堆炭火可见余烬，简单的鱼和饼，耶稣平静地把食物递给疲惫的成年门徒，手部有真实劳作质感，自然烟气、潮湿石块和木材，亲近而观察式构图，暖色火光只作为冷调清晨中的小面积视觉锚点。

### Three questions / 三次提问
**EN:** Restrained intimate two-shot of Jesus and Peter seated near a small charcoal fire after breakfast, open lakeshore behind them, natural morning light, stable camera, quiet direct eye contact, nuanced grief and compassion, no theatrical gestures, preserve exact character identities and screen direction.

**中文：** 早餐后耶稣与彼得坐在小型炭火旁的克制亲密双人镜头，身后是开阔湖岸，自然晨光，摄影机稳定，直接而安静的目光交流，呈现细腻忧伤与怜悯，不做戏剧化手势，严格保持人物身份和屏幕方向。

### Final following / 最后的跟从
**EN:** Two adult men in simple first-century garments begin walking along an open natural lakeshore in morning light, one following the other at a human pace, understated wide composition, no triumphant pose, no exaggerated sunrise, quiet forward movement.

**中文：** 两名穿着朴素第一世纪服装的成年男子在晨光中沿开阔自然湖岸开始行走，一人以平常步伐跟随另一人，克制远景构图，不摆凯旋姿势，不夸张渲染日出，安静向前移动。

## Prompt assembly / 单镜头组合

Combine the global header, relevant sequence prompt, approved character/location reference instructions, shot-specific camera and action, and global negative prompt. Never let the model invent continuity-critical details.

每个镜头组合全局提示词、对应段落提示词、已批准的人物与场景参考、镜头专属摄影机和动作要求，以及全局排除项。不要让模型自行发明影响连续性的关键细节。
