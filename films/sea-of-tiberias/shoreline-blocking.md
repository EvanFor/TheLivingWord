# Shoreline Blocking / 海岸场面调度方案

**Version / 版本:** Draft 0.1  
**Status / 状态:** Proposed geography; must be tested against storyboard / 空间提案；须结合分镜验证

## 1. Spatial principle / 空间原则

The shoreline must read as one continuous, believable place. Establish the boat's approach, the landing area, the charcoal fire, the place where the net is dragged ashore, and the later seating positions before generating coverage. Do not change the shoreline geography between the catch and breakfast.

海岸必须呈现为一个连续、可信的空间。在生成镜头前，先确定渔船靠岸方向、上岸区域、炭火位置、渔网上岸位置，以及之后人物坐下的位置。捕鱼成功到早餐之间不得改变海岸地理。

## 2. Proposed layout / 暂定布局

- **Waterline:** occupies the foreground and one side of the frame; wet stones and shallow wash establish the edge.
- **Landing zone:** directly aligned with the boat's final approach; enough room for the net to be brought ashore.
- **Fire zone:** a short walk from the water, visible from the landing zone but not placed implausibly close to the water.
- **Food:** beside the fire, simple and limited to fish and bread.
- **Jesus:** near the fire, able to address the arriving disciples and serve the meal.
- **Peter:** arrives from the water and joins the group; for the three questions, he and Jesus sit or stand in a stable two-person axis.
- **Other disciples:** remain in the same general area during breakfast, naturally present but not arranged as a formal audience.

- **水线：** 位于画面前景或一侧，以潮湿石块和浅水冲刷明确边界。
- **上岸区：** 与渔船最终靠岸方向一致，并留出足够空间把渔网拖上岸。
- **炭火区：** 距离水边一小段路程，从上岸区可以看见，但不能不合理地贴着水线。
- **食物：** 放在炭火旁，种类简单，仅有鱼和饼。
- **耶稣：** 位于炭火附近，能够迎接上岸的门徒并分发食物。
- **彼得：** 从水中上岸后加入众人；三次问答时与耶稣形成稳定的双人轴线。
- **其他门徒：** 早餐期间留在同一区域，自然在场，不要排列成正式观众席。

## 3. Screen direction / 屏幕方向

Choose one screen axis for the boat-to-shore movement and keep it throughout the sequence. Once the Jesus/Peter dialogue axis is established, do not cross it without a motivated neutral shot. Keep the fire in a consistent relative position in close-ups and reverse angles.

为船只靠岸选择一条明确的屏幕运动轴线，并贯穿整个段落。耶稣与彼得的对话轴线建立后，不要无动机越轴；若必须越轴，先使用有明确空间信息的中性镜头。所有近景和反打中，炭火的相对位置必须一致。

## 4. Sequence continuity / 段落连续性

1. The boat is offshore during the instruction and catch.
2. The beloved disciple recognizes Jesus before Peter enters the water.
3. Peter enters the water; the other disciples continue toward shore in the boat, dragging the net.
4. Jesus is already beside the charcoal fire when the boat arrives.
5. Peter reaches shore; the net is brought ashore and the fish count is established without a continuity jump.
6. The meal happens before the three questions.
7. The three questions remain in the same established fire-side geography.
8. The final “Follow me” leads away from the breakfast area along the open shore.

1. 耶稣发出指示和捕鱼成功时，渔船仍在离岸水面。
2. 所爱的门徒先认出耶稣，随后彼得才跳入水中。
3. 彼得入水后，其他门徒继续乘船靠岸并拖着渔网。
4. 渔船抵达时，耶稣已经在炭火旁。
5. 彼得上岸，渔网随后被拖上岸，鱼获数量的呈现不能发生连戏跳跃。
6. 早餐先于三次问答。
7. 三次问答始终处于已建立的炭火旁空间关系中。
8. 最后的“跟从我”引导人物沿开阔海岸离开早餐区域。

## 5. Approval checklist / 审核清单

- [ ] Boat approach and landing point are legible.
- [ ] Fire is visible from the landing zone.
- [ ] Net movement and catch continuity are physically credible.
- [ ] Peter's route from water to fire is consistent.
- [ ] Jesus and Peter's dialogue axis is stable.
- [ ] Background disciples do not jump positions between shots.
- [ ] Final walking direction is established before the ending.

- [ ] 渔船靠岸方向和上岸点清楚。
- [ ] 从上岸区能够看见炭火。
- [ ] 渔网运动和鱼获连续性符合物理规律。
- [ ] 彼得从水边到炭火旁的路线一致。
- [ ] 耶稣与彼得的对话轴线稳定。
- [ ] 背景门徒不会在镜头之间突然换位。
- [ ] 结尾行走方向提前建立。
