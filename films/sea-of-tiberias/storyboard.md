## Editorial note / 剪辑说明

The full passage needs room to breathe. Do not force it into 90 seconds by turning breakfast into a disposable montage or collapsing the three questions into one exchange. The 170-second timing is provisional and must be tested in an animatic.

整段经文需要呼吸空间。不要为了硬塞进90秒而把早餐变成可有可无的蒙太奇，也不要把三次提问压成一次问答。170秒只是暂定时长，必须通过动态分镜测试。
