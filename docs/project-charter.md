# Project Charter

## Mission

TheLivingWord is a long-term AI-assisted filmmaking project that brings biblical stories to life through cinematic storytelling. It is a home for multiple independent films, not a repository for a single short.

## Creative commitments

1. **Scripture first.** Research the relevant biblical passages before writing. Record the chosen source passages and translation.
2. **Be transparent about adaptation.** Clearly distinguish direct quotations, paraphrases, composite scenes, and invented dialogue. Never present adapted dialogue as a verbatim biblical quotation.
3. **Story before spectacle.** Prioritize understandable cause and effect, emotional truth, character motivation, and visual storytelling.
4. **Film language matters.** Plan framing, screen direction, eyelines, shot size, camera movement, sound, rhythm, and transitions as one continuous sequence.
5. **Continuity is a production requirement.** Maintain approved references for characters, costumes, locations, props, lighting, and geography.
6. **Preserve decisions.** Keep drafts and production decisions versioned. Mark what is proposed, approved, superseded, or on hold.

## Project boundaries

Each film is an independent work under `films/<film-slug>/`. Shared character and location references belong under `assets/` only when they are intended to be reused across films.

Do not merge unrelated film concepts or transfer story elements between projects without an explicit creative decision.

## First film

- Working title: **The Look (那一眼)**
- Core passage: Luke 22:54–62
- Dramatic focus: Peter's three denials, the rooster's crow, Jesus turning to look at Peter, and Peter going out and weeping bitterly.
- Central emotional idea: the failure of a person who sincerely loves Jesus when fear overwhelms him; the emotional climax is the look, not simply the third denial.
- Current status: development / draft. No script, storyboard, design, or shot is considered final unless explicitly marked approved.

## Decision status vocabulary

- **Proposed** — an idea under consideration.
- **Draft** — written or designed, but not approved.
- **Approved** — the current production reference.
- **Superseded** — replaced by a later approved decision; retain for history.
- **On hold** — intentionally paused.
